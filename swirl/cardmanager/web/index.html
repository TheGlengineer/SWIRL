Your GDEMU SD card

Put the card in your PC's reader and pick it below. SWIRL only rebuilds folder 01, the menu. Your game folders are never changed by an update.

Install SWIRL Card Manager on this PC

Adds it to the Start menu and your desktop, and to Settings > Apps so it can be removed cleanly. No administrator rights needed.

New card from scratch

Formats a card to FAT32 with the settings GDEMU likes, installs SWIRL and can copy your games over, all in one go.

Done for now?

Closes SWIRL Card Manager. It also closes by itself a few minutes after this tab is gone.

Games

Folders 02 and up, in the order GDEMU and SWIRL list them.

Art and info

Box art, descriptions and screenshots shown in SWIRL. Your own edits from a game's Edit window always win over anything filled in here.

Online art and info

Downloads the community openMenu art and info databases (about 115 MB, once) and fills in games that have none. Art already on your card and your own edits are never replaced.

Art from the game discs

Uses each disc's own artwork file (0GDTEX.PVR) for games that have no box art or VMU screen yet.

Screenshots

Shows a title screen and an in-game picture on each game's detail screen in SWIRL.

From the libretro-thumbnails Dreamcast collection, matched by game name.

Import DAT files

Optional. Point this at GDMENUCardManager's tools\openMenu\menu_data folder, or wherever you keep BOX.DAT, ICON.DAT and META.DAT. It is used at the next Update SWIRL. Leave it empty to keep the art already on the card.

Look and sound

The VMU pictures and menu music SWIRL uses. Changes go onto the Dreamcast at the next Update SWIRL.

VMU logo

SWIRL logo

Shown on the VMU when SWIRL starts and on its System screens.

Menu music

SWIRL plays its own theme unless you pick your own music.

VMU screens from the games

Boots each game for a few seconds in a hidden emulator on this PC and keeps the picture the game puts on the VMU. About 10 seconds per game.

Collections

Your own groups of games, like Couch co-op. They appear in SWIRL's Collections tab after Update SWIRL.

GDEMU settings

GDEMU.INI on the card. These are saved straight away and do not need Update SWIRL.

Health and preview

Check the card for problems, or try the menu before you write it.

Card health check

Checks the format, the folder numbering and that every game image is complete. It only reads the card.

Preview in Flycast

Builds the exact menu this card would get and opens it in the emulator built into this app. Nothing is written to the card.

Backups

Copy the whole card to your PC, and manage the old menus and removed games kept on the card.

Back up this card to your PC

Copies every game, the menu, your art and edits and GDEMU.INI into a dated folder. To put a backup onto a card later, use New card from scratch and pick the backup folder.

Kept on the card

Every install moves the previous menu here, and removed games wait here until you delete them.

SWIRLCard Managerv__VERSION__

Sets up and looks after a GDEMU SD card for the SWIRL Dreamcast menu. By Glen Huszar, github.com/TheGlengineer

Supported files

Disc images
GDI, CDI, MDS, CCD
Archives
ZIP, 7Z, RAR (including multi part RAR). Unpacked straight onto the card, no temporary copy on your PC
Pictures
PNG, JPG, GIF for box art, VMU screens, screenshots and the menu logo
Music
MP3 or WAV, converted for the Dreamcast

Keyboard shortcuts

F1
This window
Ctrl F
Find a game
F5
Read the card again
Enter
In a folder box: open that folder
Esc
Close a window

Managing games

Add
Games, Add games: tick games, folders, disc files or archives. Games already on the card are skipped
Remove
Tick games, then Remove selected. They go to SWIRL_BACKUP on the card, not straight to the bin
Reorder
Drag rows or covers, then Save new order
Views
List or Covers, with a size slider for covers
Multi disc
Discs of one game are grouped. Put discs together fixes their order

Names, art and info

Tidy names
Proper titles from the Redump list, matched by each disc's serial
Edit
Click a game to change its name, box art, VMU screen, screenshots and details. Names you type are never overwritten
Databases
Art and info downloads the openMenu box art and game info
Screenshots
Get online in the Edit window, or for every game at once
VMU screens
Capture VMU screens boots each game briefly and keeps the picture it draws

Look, sound and collections

Music
Use your own track, or go back to the SWIRL theme at any time
Logo
Your own picture for the menu and VMU
Collections
Your own groups of games, shown in SWIRL
Update SWIRL
Rebuilds the menu in folder 01 with every change. Game folders are never touched

Card tools and backups

New card
Formats a card for GDEMU and fills it with games you pick, or with a backup
Health check
Checks the format, free space, the menu, folder numbering gaps, incomplete discs and leftover system files
GDEMU settings
Edits GDEMU.INI
Backup to PC
Copies the whole card into a dated folder. Updates only copy what changed
Old menus
Every install keeps the previous menu, ready to restore

Region, VGA and boot options

Every game boots region free and in VGA, with nothing changed on the card. To set one game differently, open it on the Dreamcast and press X for Options: Region (game default, Japan, USA, Europe), Video (Force VGA or game default) and Boot (straight in, boot animation, SEGA screen). The choice is saved for that game.
